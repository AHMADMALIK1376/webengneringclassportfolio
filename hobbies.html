Online game

PUBG Mobile

PUBG Mobile is a multiplayer battle-royale game where up to 100 players parachute onto an island, collect weapons and supplies, and fight to be the last player or squad standing. The safe zone keeps shrinking, so you have to plan your route and move carefully.

I usually play in a squad of four with my university friends. It has taught me a lot about communication, quick decision-making and teamwork under pressure.

Physical game

Cricket

Cricket is a bat-and-ball game played between two teams of eleven players. One team bats and tries to score runs, while the other team bowls and fields to get the batters out. The team with more runs wins.

Every weekend my friends and I play tape-ball cricket in the ground near our home. I enjoy batting the most, and the game keeps me active and refreshed after a long week of classes.
