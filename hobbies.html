Physical game

Skating

Skating is gliding on wheels by pushing off with one foot and balancing on the board or skates. It looks easy, but it takes a lot of practice to keep your balance, turn smoothly and stop safely.

Skating is my favourite way to stay active. It clears my mind after long hours of coding, and every time I learn a new move it feels like a small win.
